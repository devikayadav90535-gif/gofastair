FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What information is needed for a quote?

Pickup and delivery locations, shipment type, approximate weight, dimensions where available, and any urgency or special handling requirement.

Do you provide door-to-door service?

Door-to-door service options are available depending on route, shipment type and serviceability. Confirm the applicable option when requesting a quote.

Can I ship commercial or bulk consignments?

Yes. Use the Business / Bulk Shipping enquiry and include expected frequency, average weight and destination details so the team can assess the requirement.

Is the tracking form live?

This starter includes the front-end tracking experience only. Connect it to your actual AWB/tracking provider or internal system before publishing it as a live tracking service.
